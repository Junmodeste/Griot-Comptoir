# Sample ledger pages

`synthetic_page_*.jpg` are generated by `scripts/make_synthetic_pages.py`: invented names, products and
amounts, rendered with a handwriting font. Two lines carry deliberate arithmetic errors
(page 01 line 6, page 03 line 6) to exercise the verification layer.

Real pages must be anonymised (customer names replaced, faces and phone numbers removed) before being
added here. Never commit an original photo.
