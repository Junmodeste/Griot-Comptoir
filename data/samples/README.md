# Sample ledger pages

- `synthetic_page_*.jpg` are generated by `scripts/make_synthetic_pages.py`: invented names, products and
  amounts, rendered with a handwriting font. Two lines carry deliberate arithmetic errors
  (page 01 line 6, page 03 line 6) to exercise the verification layer.
- `real_page_*.jpg` are 15 pages handwritten on paper by several volunteers in Côte d'Ivoire (Nov 2026),
  photographed with a phone and sent over WhatsApp (compressed, about 600x1080). The content is
  fictitious: market lists, shop restocking, small-shop sales, a debt line. Some pages are rotated,
  on squared paper, or partly covered by a finger; several have page totals that do not match the lines.

No real customer data: never commit an original photo of a real shop's notebook.
